**LINE WEIGHT ON THE SIX SVG DRAWINGS — reset to Zaid's own level (2026-10-20, published).**

- **Per-stroke ink was the wrong control.** The six measured mid-band against the ten raster drawings
  (`0.149-0.674`) while plainly looking lighter. The tell was the darkest pixel — **`55-91` on the SVG six
  against `126-179` on the raster ten**. Vector gives a thin near-black line; raster gives a fat grey one.
  Same ink, opposite read. **The eye reads stroke WIDTH; per-stroke ink measures MASS.** This is the exact
  inverse of the `Liga T`/`Liga W` case, where thin-but-dark read as too dark.
- **Now a uniform `0.700` device-px stroke** — Zaid's own mean. Per-stroke `0.952-1.165` sits inside his

  `stroke-width 5` renders `0.619-0.846px` — a 1.37x spread. Used his level held uniform instead.
- **Frames held byte-identical**, so stroke width was the only variable. `geo` grew `+0.02` to `+0.20` pt
  because a thicker stroke widens the ink bbox; the geometry did not move.
